# Understand Workspace settings

Open **Workspace** for workspace-level configuration. The current UI includes **Workspace profile**, **Team members**, **API keys**, **Security & privacy**, **Plan & billing**, and access to **White Label** settings.

Management actions are restricted by backend role checks where applicable.
