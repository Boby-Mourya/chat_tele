# Choose the right member role

Use least privilege when inviting or managing workspace members.

## Before you start

Workspace **Owner** or **Admin** access is required.

## Choose based on responsibilities

- **Admin** — needs to manage agents, knowledge, integrations, or workspace configuration.
- **Member** — needs to operate conversations/leads but not administer the whole workspace.
- **Analyst** — needs reporting/read access without operational configuration changes.
- **Owner** — reserve for the workspace owner/highest-level responsibility.

## What happens next

Backend permissions apply immediately according to the stored membership role.

## Troubleshooting

If someone needs one restricted action occasionally, prefer having an Admin perform it rather than permanently over-privileging the account.
