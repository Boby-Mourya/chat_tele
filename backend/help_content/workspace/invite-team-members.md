# Invite team members

Invite another person into the current workspace from the Team area.

## Before you start

Workspace **Owner** or **Admin** access is required.

## Steps

1. Open **Workspace** and the team/member section.
2. Select **Invite a team member**.
3. Enter the person's **Email address**.
4. Choose **Admin**, **Member**, or **Analyst**.
5. Select **Send invitation**.
6. Review the member list for the new invitation/member state.

## Security

Choose the least-privileged role that can perform the person's real job. Do not give Admin access only to avoid a permissions error.

## Troubleshooting

If the invitation fails, verify the email address and your Owner/Admin role.
