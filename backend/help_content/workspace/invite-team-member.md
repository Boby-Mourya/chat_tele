# Invite a team member and choose a role

1. Open **Workspace**.
2. Find **Team members**.
3. Choose **Invite member**.
4. Enter the requested teammate information.
5. Choose the role that matches the person's responsibilities.
6. Send the invitation.

Use the least privilege that still allows the teammate to do their work.
