# White-label settings

White-label settings let an Owner/Admin apply workspace branding and support contact information.

## Before you start

Workspace **Owner** or **Admin** access is required.

## What you can configure

The current workspace settings include company/branding values such as logo, colors, company name, support email, support URL, and custom branding/domain fields supported by the White Label page.

## Steps

1. Open **Workspace → White Label**.
2. Update only the branding/support fields your organization owns.
3. Save the changes.
4. Reopen Help and the customer-facing surfaces to verify the expected contact/branding behavior.

## Troubleshooting

If a support destination does not change immediately, refresh the workspace settings and verify the saved white-label values.
