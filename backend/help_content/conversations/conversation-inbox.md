# Understand the Conversations inbox

Open **Conversations** to review visitor threads handled by your agents. Select a conversation to read messages, channel, visitor details when available, sentiment, and current state.

Common states are **open**, **resolved**, and **escalated**. Grounded assistant messages can include citations.
