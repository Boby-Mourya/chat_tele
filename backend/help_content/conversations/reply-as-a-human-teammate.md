# Reply as a human teammate

A human teammate can take part in a conversation when the workspace role has conversation write permission.

## Before you start

Workspace **Owner**, **Admin**, or **Member** access is required for the action steps.

## Steps

1. Open **Conversations** and select the conversation.
2. Read the recent messages and any escalation context.
3. Enter the human response in the reply composer.
4. Send the response.
5. Continue the conversation or resolve it once the customer's issue is complete.

## Good practice

Do not ask the customer to resend secrets that should never be collected. Keep human responses consistent with workspace policies.

## Troubleshooting

If sending is blocked, confirm your role is Owner, Admin, or Member and that the conversation is still available to the workspace.
