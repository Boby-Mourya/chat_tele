# Resolve a conversation

Resolve a conversation when the customer's current issue is complete and no further action is expected.

## Before you start

Workspace **Owner**, **Admin**, or **Member** access is required for the action steps.

## Steps

1. Open the conversation.
2. Confirm the last answer or human response addresses the issue.
3. Use the resolve action to move the conversation to **resolved**.
4. Reopen or continue handling only when new work is required and the UI/backend allows it.

## What happens next

Resolved conversations remain part of reporting/history; resolution does not mean the record is immediately deleted.

## Troubleshooting

If you cannot change the state, check your workspace role and refresh the conversation before retrying.
