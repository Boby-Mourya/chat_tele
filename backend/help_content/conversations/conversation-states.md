# Open, resolved, and escalated states

- **Open**: active or still needs attention.
- **Resolved**: considered complete.
- **Escalated**: needs human attention.

Owners, Admins, and Members can perform permitted conversation write actions in the current backend. Analysts should treat conversation guidance as read-oriented.
