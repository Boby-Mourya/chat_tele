# Open and read a conversation

The **Conversations** inbox lets authenticated workspace users inspect customer/agent message history and current state.

## Before you start

This guide is available to all authenticated workspace roles.

## Steps

1. Open **Conversations** from the sidebar.
2. Select the conversation you want to review.
3. Read messages in chronological order and note whether they came from the visitor, assistant, or a human teammate.
4. Check the conversation state and any escalation indicators.
5. Review citations on grounded assistant answers when citations are present.

## What happens next

Members, Admins, and Owners can use the operational reply/resolve actions allowed by the backend. Analysts should treat the inbox as read-oriented.
