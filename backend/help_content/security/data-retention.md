# Data retention

Retention settings determine how long conversation/operational data is kept according to workspace policy.

## Before you start

Workspace **Owner** or **Admin** access is required.

## What to review

- Agent/deployment privacy settings.
- Workspace policy for conversations, leads, and support data.
- Whether a deleted agent's conversation history is retained according to the data policy, as stated by the current delete confirmation.

## Important

Retention is separate from UI visibility and separate from deleting one agent configuration. Follow your organization's policy and applicable requirements.

## Troubleshooting

If you need to understand what a specific deployment currently retains, review its configured policy and do not assume deleting a frontend record erases every related historical event.
