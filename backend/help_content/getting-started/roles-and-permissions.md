# Understand Northstar roles and permissions

Northstar currently uses four workspace roles: **Owner**, **Admin**, **Member**, and **Analyst**.

- **Owner / Admin** can manage agents, knowledge, integrations, and workspace settings.
- **Member** can perform permitted operational conversation and lead work.
- **Analyst** is mainly read-oriented.

Permissions are enforced by the backend. If an action is blocked, confirm your current workspace role and sign in again if the session role may be stale.
