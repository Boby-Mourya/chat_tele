# Welcome to Northstar AI

Northstar brings your AI agents, knowledge, conversations, leads, analytics, integrations, deployment, workspace settings, and Help into one authenticated workspace.

## Main navigation

1. **Overview** shows a workspace summary.
2. **Agents** contains your AI agents.
3. **Conversations** and **Leads** support day-to-day operations.
4. **Analytics** shows performance.
5. **Integrations** and **Deploy** handle channels and installation.
6. **Workspace** contains team and workspace settings.
7. **Help** contains official Northstar product guidance.

What you can change depends on your workspace role.
