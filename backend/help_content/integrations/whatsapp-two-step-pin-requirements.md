# WhatsApp two-step PIN requirements

Meta can require a WhatsApp two-step verification PIN during business phone-number setup or management.

## Before you start

Workspace **Owner** or **Admin** access is required.

## What to do

1. Start or manage the WhatsApp connection from **Integrations**.
2. When Meta requests the two-step PIN, use the PIN configured for that WhatsApp Business phone number.
3. Do not store the PIN in Northstar agent instructions, knowledge sources, Help requests, or source control.
4. Finish the Meta flow and verify Northstar reports the connection as active.

## Troubleshooting

If the PIN is unknown, recover/manage it through the appropriate Meta/WhatsApp Business account process. Northstar should not display or recover that secret for you.
