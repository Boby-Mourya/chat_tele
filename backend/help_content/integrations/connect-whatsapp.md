# Connect WhatsApp Business

## Before you start

You need an Owner/Admin role plus the Meta prerequisites required by your deployment.

1. Open **Integrations** and choose WhatsApp.
2. Start the configured Meta Embedded Signup flow.
3. Select the requested WhatsApp Business assets.
4. Complete the signup callback in Northstar.
5. Provide the required two-step verification PIN when prompted.
6. Associate the connection with the intended agent and confirm status.

Never paste Meta access tokens into Help requests or knowledge.
