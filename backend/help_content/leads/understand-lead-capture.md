# Understand lead capture

Northstar can store lead/contact information collected through supported conversation flows so workspace users can follow up.

## Before you start

This guide is available to all authenticated workspace roles.

## What to expect

A lead record belongs to the workspace and can include contact details, status, consent-related information, and the conversation context exposed by the current Leads UI.

Use the **Leads** page to review captured records rather than copying customer contact details into agent instructions or Help articles.

## Privacy

Only collect information needed for the stated customer interaction. Consent and privacy settings should match the deployment's policy.

## Troubleshooting

If no leads appear, verify the active agent/deployment actually uses a lead-capture flow and that the relevant conversation reached the capture step.
