# Update a lead status

Owners, Admins, and Members can perform permitted lead write actions.

1. Open **Leads**.
2. Find the captured contact.
3. Use the available status control.
4. Choose the status that matches your workflow.
5. Confirm the update.

If the backend returns a permission error, verify your workspace role.
