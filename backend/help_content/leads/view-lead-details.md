# View lead details

Use the **Leads** page to inspect a captured contact without changing the agent itself.

## Before you start

This guide is available to all authenticated workspace roles.

## Steps

1. Open **Leads**.
2. Select or expand the lead shown in the current interface.
3. Review the available contact fields, current lead status, timestamps, and consent-related information.
4. Use the linked context where the UI exposes it to understand why the lead was captured.

## Good practice

Do not export or copy personal information into unrelated systems unless your workspace policy permits it.

## Troubleshooting

If expected details are blank, they may never have been collected from the visitor; Northstar should not fabricate missing contact data.
