# Consent and lead information

Consent settings help explain and govern how visitor information is processed before or while a lead is captured.

## Before you start

This guide is available to all authenticated workspace roles.

## Review these areas

- Agent/deployment consent settings and consent message.
- **Privacy policy URL** when required.
- Whether the lead record actually contains a consent indicator.
- The workspace's data-retention expectations.

## Important

Consent configuration is not legal advice. Configure the deployment according to your organization's policy and applicable requirements.

## Troubleshooting

If lead data exists without the expected consent flow, review the deployment configuration before continuing to collect new information.
