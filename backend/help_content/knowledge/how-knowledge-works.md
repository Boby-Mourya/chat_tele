# How Northstar knowledge works

Knowledge provides evidence that an agent can retrieve before generation.

1. An Owner/Admin adds a source.
2. Northstar stores and processes it.
3. Content is extracted and split into chunks.
4. Embeddings are generated.
5. Approved content becomes retrievable for that agent.
6. Chat combines retrieval with grounded generation and citations.

Customer knowledge is separate from Northstar Help documentation.
