# I received a permission error

Northstar enforces roles in the backend. Owner/Admin manage agents, knowledge, integrations, and workspace configuration. Member supports permitted conversation/lead operations. Analyst is primarily read-oriented.

If an action should be available, sign in again to refresh a stale role and confirm the workspace membership.
