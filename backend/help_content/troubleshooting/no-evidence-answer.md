# The bot says it does not have enough evidence

Northstar is designed to avoid fabricating unsupported answers. This usually means retrieval did not find sufficiently relevant approved knowledge.

Owners/Admins should verify the needed source exists, is Ready, and states the answer clearly. Improve the official source rather than weakening grounding to force an unsupported answer.
